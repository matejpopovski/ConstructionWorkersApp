# Construction Gossip Support

For support, contact support@constructiongossip.app.

Include:

- Your username
- The email used for the account
- A short description of the issue
- Screenshots if helpful
- The device and iOS version

Safety issues, harassment, private information, impersonation, or illegal content should be reported in the app using the report menu where possible.

Account deletion is available in the app from Profile > Privacy Settings > Delete Account.
