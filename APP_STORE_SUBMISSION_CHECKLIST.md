# App Store Submission Checklist

Use this checklist before submitting Construction Gossip directly to App Review.

## Can Be Done In Xcode

- Confirm the final app name is `Construction Gossip`.
- Confirm the final bundle ID is registered in Apple Developer.
- Confirm signing uses the paid Apple Developer team.
- Archive a Release build from Xcode.
- Upload the archive to App Store Connect.
- Provide a reviewer account that can log in and test posts, comments, chat, reporting, blocking, and account deletion.

## App Store Connect Metadata

- App name: Construction Gossip
- Subtitle: Construction job reviews
- Category: Social Networking
- Age rating: 17+ recommended because users can post public workplace complaints, photos, and user-generated content.
- Price: Free unless monetization is added later.
- Support URL: required
- Privacy Policy URL: required
- Terms of Use URL: recommended
- App Review notes: use `APP_REVIEW_NOTES.md`

## Review-Critical Product Requirements

- Account creation works.
- Login works.
- Account deletion is available in-app.
- User-generated content can be reported.
- Users can block other users.
- Anonymous posts do not reveal identity to other users.
- Photos can be attached and are compressed before storage.
- External share links use an app deep link.
- No placeholder screens or broken buttons are visible.

## Still Needs Owner Action

- Paid Apple Developer Program enrollment.
- App Store Connect app record.
- Assign a person to monitor support requests and the moderation queue.
- Confirm the final hosted legal URLs load before submission.
- Assign a person to the moderation queue and monitor the support inbox.
