# Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 10, 2026

Construction Gossip is a mobile app for construction workers to share workplace experiences, employer reviews, pay context, safety notes, comments, messages, and profile information.

## Information Collected

The app may collect account information such as username, email address, password credentials, profile photo, name, city, state, job or position, company or employer, union status, experience, bio, privacy preferences, posts, comments, likes, messages, reports, blocks, and photos uploaded by the user.

The app may also store technical information needed to operate the service, secure accounts, moderate content, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used to create and manage accounts, show profiles, publish posts and comments, send messages, display search results, support sharing, enforce privacy choices, process reports, block users, prevent abuse, improve the service, and comply with legal obligations.

## User Content

Posts, comments, profile fields, and photos may be visible to other users depending on privacy settings and whether a post is anonymous. Anonymous posts are designed to hide the author identity from other users, but the service may retain internal records needed for safety, moderation, legal compliance, and abuse prevention.

## Private Information

The app does not request a street address, phone number, Social Security number, contacts, or precise location. Users should not post private personal information about themselves or others in reviews, comments, profile text, or messages.

## Sharing

Users may share app content using system share features or in-app messaging. Shared links may identify a specific post.

## Reports And Blocks

Users can report posts and comments and can block other users. Reports and block records may be retained to operate safety and moderation features.

## Account Deletion

Users can permanently delete their account from inside the app. Deletion removes the authentication account and associated database records. The app also requests removal of media files owned by that account before deleting it.

## Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect user information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
