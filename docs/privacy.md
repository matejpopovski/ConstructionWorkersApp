**Effective date: June 10, 2026**

Construction Gossip is a mobile app for construction workers to share workplace experiences, employer reviews, pay context, safety notes, comments, messages, and profile information.

## Information We Collect

The app may collect account information such as username, email address, authentication credentials, profile photo, name, city, state, job or position, company or employer, union status, experience, bio, privacy preferences, job reviews, comments, likes, follows, private messages, reports, blocks, and photos uploaded by the user.

Supabase processes authentication, database, and file-storage information needed to operate the service. We may also process technical information needed to secure accounts, moderate content, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.

## How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts, display profiles and feeds, publish reviews and comments, send private messages, provide search and sharing, enforce privacy choices, process reports and blocks, recover accounts, prevent abuse, improve reliability, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

## Visibility and User Content

Job reviews, comments, selected profile fields, and their photos may be visible to other signed-in users. Private messages and message photos are limited to conversation participants.

Anonymous reviews hide the author's identity from other users, but the service retains the account relationship for safety, moderation, abuse prevention, and legal compliance.

## Information You Should Not Post

The app does not request a street address, phone number, Social Security number, contacts, or precise location. Users should not post private personal information about themselves or others in reviews, comments, profiles, or messages.

## Sharing

Users may share app content through system sharing or in-app messaging. Shared links may identify a specific review.

## Reports and Blocks

Users can report reviews, comments, replies, and profiles, and can block other users. Reports and block records may be retained to operate safety and moderation features.

## Data Retention and Account Deletion

Data is retained while an account is active and as reasonably necessary for security, legal obligations, moderation, and dispute resolution. Users can permanently delete their account inside the app from Profile > Settings. Deletion removes the authentication account and associated database records and requests deletion of media owned by the account.

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure.

## Your Choices

Users can change profile visibility, report content, block users, delete their own content, and permanently delete their account from inside the app.

## Contact

For privacy questions or deletion support, use the [Construction Gossip support page](support.html).

## Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the latest published version.
